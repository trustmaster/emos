## Days 1–30 — Learn

_Bias to listening. Change nothing structural unless it's on fire._

| Focus | Specifics | Done |
|-------|-----------|------|
| People | 1-on-1 with every team member; ask what they'd change | [ ] |
| System | Own the on-call shadow rotation; read the last 3 post-mortems | [ ] |
| Work | Understand the roadmap and where it's actually blocked | [ ] |
| Stakeholders | Meet the 3–5 people whose opinion of this team matters | [ ] |
| Cadence | Attend every recurring meeting once before changing any | [ ] |

**Checkpoint (day 30):** what surprised them, what they'd change, what they need.

## Days 31–60 — Diagnose

| Focus | Specifics | Done |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Team health | First `team-health` pass; name the top two problems | [ ] |
| Delivery | Form a view on whether commitments are realistic | [ ] |
| Talent | Skill/will read on each report; spot the flight risks | [ ] |
| Process | Keep, kill, or change one ritual — with the team's input | [ ] |

**Checkpoint (day 60):** their diagnosis, and where you disagree with it.

## Days 61–90 — Act

| Focus | Specifics | Done |
|-------|-----------|------|
| One visible change | Ship the fix to the clearest problem | [ ] |
| Plan | Own the next quarter's plan end to end | [ ] |
| Feedback | Give one piece of hard feedback; receive one | [ ] |
| Autonomy | Run their own staff/planning meeting without you | [ ] |

**Checkpoint (day 90):** against the success criteria above.
